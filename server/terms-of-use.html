1 🩺 About VitalSync

VitalSync is a personal health aggregator app developed by akorir, MSN, RN, CCRN-CMC — a critical care nurse with advanced clinical training. The app enables you to track, visualise, and manage your own health metrics entirely on your device, including:

  • Blood pressure and pulse readings
  • Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Sleep duration and quality
  • Body composition (weight, BMI, body fat)
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂)
  • ECG / electrocardiogram data
  • Respiratory rate
  • Medications and supplement tracking
  • Doctor-ready PDF and CSV reports

📱 VitalSync is a personal wellness tool, not a medical device. It is not intended to replace professional medical care, diagnosis, or treatment.

VitalSync does not upload, transmit, or share your personal health information with any external server, cloud service, or third party.

What This Means for You

  • You retain full ownership of all data you create within the app.
  • No account or sign-up is required to use VitalSync.
  • Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all stored data.

VitalSync can optionally read data from Apple HealthKit (iOS) and Google Health Connect (Android). Health data accessed from these platforms is read locally on your device and is never transmitted externally. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device Settings.
