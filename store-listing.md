## Description

**Built by a critical care nurse. Grounded in peer-reviewed research.**

VitalSync Health Tracker was designed in the ICU — not in a boardroom. Every feature reflects the clinical standards that nurses and physicians use to interpret vital signs, assess medication safety, and identify health patterns before they become emergencies.

---

**EVIDENCE-BASED HEALTH TRACKING**

VitalSync doesn't just log your numbers — it interprets them. Correlations between blood pressure, sleep quality, glucose, and nutrition are grounded in published research from the American Heart Association (AHA), American Diabetes Association (ADA), American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM), and the CDC. Every insight links to the peer-reviewed source so you can read the evidence yourself.

---

**WHAT YOU CAN TRACK**

• Blood pressure, heart rate, and oxygen saturation (SpO₂)
• Blood glucose and projected A1C estimates
• Medications, dosing schedules, and drug interaction alerts
• Nutrition — point your camera at any meal for USDA-verified macros, calories, and glycemic load
• Sleep quality and AASM-aligned sleep stage analysis (NeoSleep)
• Lab results and uploaded documents
• EKG waveforms and rhythm trends
• Weight, BMI, and body composition

---

**CLINICAL-GRADE REPORTS**

Generate a structured PDF health summary — vitals, labs, medications, and trends — formatted for your next doctor's appointment. Reports include evidence-based references and are designed to be readable by any clinician, not just you.

---

**YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS**

Everything is stored on your device. No cloud accounts required. No health data sold, shared, or monetized — ever. VitalSync is built on the principle that your health information belongs to you and your care team, no one else.

---

**PREMIUM FEATURES**

• Unlimited vital signs, glucose, and lab tracking
• Advanced correlation engine and pattern detection
• Projected A1C estimates from glucose trends
• Smart medication reminders and refill alerts
• Appointment management and reminders
• Clinical-grade PDF health reports
• EKG waveform viewer and rhythm trends
• Bluetooth device sync (blood pressure monitors, scales)
• Unlimited data history
• Priority support

---

**FREE PLAN INCLUDES**

• Blood pressure, glucose, and medication logging
• 30-day data history
• Basic trend charts
• Medication tracker

---

**PRIVACY**

VitalSync does not require an account. All data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. No analytics, no ad tracking, no data brokers. Read our full Privacy Policy at vitalsync.health/privacy.
