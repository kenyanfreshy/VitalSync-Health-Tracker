# Welcome to VitalSync Support

**Thank you for downloading VitalSync — the privacy-first health aggregator built for people who take their health seriously.**

VitalSync brings together your blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, ECG, sleep, medications, and nutrition into one beautifully designed app — entirely on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No subscriptions. Your health data belongs to you, and only you.

---

## What VitalSync Does

VitalSync is a comprehensive personal health hub designed to help you track, understand, and act on your most important clinical metrics. Whether you are managing a chronic condition, optimising athletic performance, or simply staying informed about your body, VitalSync gives you the tools that were previously only available in a clinical setting.

Every reading you log — every blood pressure measurement, every glucose entry, every ECG scan — stays stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. It is never uploaded to a server, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. You own your data completely.

---

## Key Features

**Vitals Tracking** — Log and trend blood pressure, heart rate, SpO₂, HRV, and body temperature with clinical-grade charting and 7-day or 30-day history views.

**Blood Sugar Monitoring** — Track fasting, post-meal, and bedtime glucose readings with a projected A1C estimate and colour-coded range indicators.

**ECG Scan** — Perform a single-lead ECG scan directly from your device and receive an instant rhythm classification with HRV analysis.

**Sleep Analysis** — Review your nightly sleep stages (Deep, Core, REM, Awake), efficiency score, and 7-night quality trends.

**Medications** — Manage your current prescriptions with dosage details, prescriber notes, and daily adherence tracking.

**Nutrition & Macro Intelligence** — Log meals and track protein, carbohydrates, fat, and calories against your personalised goals using evidence-based dietary protocols including Balanced, Ketogenic, Vegan, Mediterranean, Diabetic-Friendly, and more.

**Bio-Aligned Recipes** — Browse clinically aligned recipes filtered by your active diet protocol, with full macro breakdowns per serving.

**Evidence-Based Insights** — Receive personalised health insights derived from AHA, WHO, and ISSN guidelines, updated in real time based on your logged data.

**Daily Readiness Score** — Start each day with an at-a-glance readiness score that synthesises your sleep, HRV, and vitals into a single actionable number.

## Your Privacy, Guaranteed

VitalSync was built on a single non-negotiable principle: **your health data never leaves your device.**

- No cloud sync
- No server uploads
- No accounts required
- No third-party data sharing
- You control all exports

When you export a Doctor Report PDF to share with your physician, that is the only moment any data leaves your device — and you initiate it manually, every time.
