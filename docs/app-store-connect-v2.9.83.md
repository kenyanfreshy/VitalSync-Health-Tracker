## SECTION 5 — FULL APP DESCRIPTION

```
VitalSync is your personal health and wellness hub — a beautifully organized space to log, track, and understand the metrics that matter most to you.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS

Log blood pressure, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen (SpO₂), body temperature, respiratory rate, blood glucose, weight, and sleep — all in one place. VitalSync reads from and writes to Apple Health, so your data stays connected across your devices and apps.

UNDERSTAND YOUR TRENDS

The dashboard surfaces your most important vitals at a glance, with sparkline charts and trend indicators that show you where you are and where you're heading. Tap any metric to explore your full history, view weekly and monthly averages, and spot patterns over time.

MEDICATION MANAGEMENT

Never miss a dose. VitalSync's medication tracker lets you log your prescriptions, set schedules, track refills, and monitor adherence over time. Smart reminders notify you when it's time to take a medication, and the refill card on your dashboard shows exactly when to call your pharmacy.

IMMUNIZATION RECORDS

Keep your vaccination history organized and shareable. Log every immunization with date, lot number, manufacturer, dose, and administering provider. Set a next-dose reminder, and VitalSync will surface a smart reminder on your dashboard 48 hours and 24 hours before it's due. Generate a shareable vaccination certificate PDF directly from the app.

NUTRITION & MEAL LOGGING

Log meals manually, scan a barcode, or use your camera to analyze a meal photo. VitalSync calculates calories, macros, sugar, and glycemic load, and tracks your nutrition trends alongside your vitals so you can see how what you eat affects how you feel.

SLEEP TRACKING

Log your sleep sessions or sync them from Apple Health. View your sleep duration, quality trends, and how your sleep patterns correlate with your energy and vitals.

APPOINTMENTS & MEDICAL HISTORY

Organize upcoming appointments, log your medical and surgical history, track allergies, and keep your health records in one secure place — ready to share with your care team.

EKG FROM APPLE HEALTH

VitalSync reads your EKG recordings from Apple Health and displays them as a waveform viewer with a rhythm summary. Review your heart rhythm history and share a PDF export with your physician.

HEALTH REPORTS

Generate a comprehensive PDF health report covering your vitals, medications, labs, and immunizations — formatted for easy sharing with your doctor.

APPLE HEALTH INTEGRATION

VitalSync integrates deeply with Apple Health. It reads blood pressure, heart rate, HRV, blood glucose, weight, body fat, SpO₂, sleep analysis, steps, active energy, workouts, and EKG recordings. It writes back your manually entered vitals so your Apple Health record stays complete.

BLE DEVICE SUPPORT (PREMIUM)

Connect Bluetooth-enabled clinical monitors, glucometers, blood pressure cuffs, and smart scales directly to VitalSync. Readings sync automatically to your dashboard and Apple Health.

─────────────────────────────
VITALSYNC PREMIUM
─────────────────────────────

Unlock the full VitalSync experience with a Premium subscription:

• EKG Analysis — View your EKG waveform and rhythm summary from Apple Health recordings.
• Projected A1C — See your estimated A1C trend based on your continuous glucose readings.
• Smart Reminders — Intelligent reminders for medications, hydration, vitals, and appointments.
• Health Report Export — Generate and export detailed PDF health reports to share with your physician.
• Advanced Correlations — Discover patterns between your vitals, nutrition, sleep, and activity.
• On-Device Insights — Apple LLM powered wellness insights analyzed entirely on your device — no data leaves your phone. (Android: Gemini powered wellness insights.)
• Full Vital History — Access your complete vital history beyond the 30-day free window.
• Advanced Nutrition — Full meal history, macro trends, and nutrition correlations with your vitals.
• Dashboard Customization — Rearrange, pin, and personalize every card on your dashboard.
• BLE Device Support — Connect and manage Bluetooth health devices and data sources.
• Medications Tracker — Full medication management with schedules, refills, and adherence tracking.
• Appointments Tracker — Manage medical appointments and reminders.
• Scan a Meal — Instantly analyze any meal with your camera.
• Scan Barcode — Scan packaged food barcodes to log accurate nutrition data from the USDA database.

Try Premium free for 14 days. No charge until your trial ends.

─────────────────────────────
YOUR PRIVACY

All your health data is stored locally on your device. VitalSync does not sell, share, or transmit your personal health information to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties. You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Privacy → Delete All My Data.

VitalSync is built for people, not profit.

## SECTION 8 — APP REVIEW NOTES

```
REVIEWER NOTES — VitalSync 2.9.84

Thank you for reviewing VitalSync. The following notes are provided to assist with your review.

─── HEALTHKIT USAGE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VitalSync uses HealthKit to read and write the following data types:

READ: Blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood glucose, body mass (weight), body fat percentage, lean body mass, height, step count, oxygen saturation (SpO₂), body temperature, respiratory rate, sleep analysis, active energy burned, walking/running distance, cycling distance, workout sessions, and electrocardiogram (EKG) recordings.

WRITE: Heart rate, HRV, blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), blood glucose, body mass (weight), oxygen saturation (SpO₂), sleep analysis.

All HealthKit permissions are requested at onboarding via the standard iOS HealthKit authorization sheet. The app functions without HealthKit access — manual entry is always available.

─── BLOOD PRESSURE PERMISSION FLOW ────────────────────────────────────────────

To grant VitalSync access to Blood Pressure data in Apple Health:

  Apple Health → Data Access & Devices → VitalSync → Blood Pressure

Blood pressure data is read-only by default. The app will prompt the user for HealthKit authorization during onboarding. If the user declines, they can grant access manually via the path above.

─── PREMIUM GATING BEHAVIOR ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

VitalSync has a free tier and a Premium subscription tier. The following features are gated behind Premium:

  • EKG Analysis (waveform viewer and rhythm summary)
  • Projected A1C (estimated A1C trend from glucose readings)
  • Smart Reminders (medication, hydration, vitals, appointment reminders)
  • Health Report Export (PDF generation and sharing)
  • Advanced Correlations (vitals/nutrition/sleep correlation analysis)
  • On-Device Insights (Apple LLM powered wellness insights on iOS)
  • Full Vital History (beyond 30-day free window)
  • Advanced Nutrition (macro trends and nutrition correlations)
  • Dashboard Customization (drag-and-drop card arrangement)
  • BLE Device Support (Bluetooth health device connectivity)
  • Medications Tracker (full medication management)
  • Appointments Tracker (appointment management)
  • Scan a Meal (camera-based meal analysis)
  • Scan Barcode (barcode-based nutrition logging)

When a free user taps a gated feature, a bottom sheet is presented explaining the feature and offering a "Start Free Trial" button. No content is hidden without explanation.

─── TRIAL ACTIVATION BEHAVIOR ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

A 14-day free trial is available for new subscribers. The trial is activated via the standard StoreKit purchase flow. During the trial, all Premium features are fully unlocked. The trial does not auto-charge until the 14-day period ends. A local push notification is sent 24 hours before the trial expires as a reminder.

─── EKG GATING ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The EKG screen reads EKG recordings from Apple Health. It is gated behind Premium. Free users who tap the EKG tab are shown the Premium gate sheet. Premium users who have EKG recordings in Apple Health will see the waveform viewer and rhythm summary. If no EKG recordings are present in Apple Health, the screen shows an empty state with instructions to record an EKG using Apple Watch.

─── EXPORT / REPORT GATING ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Health Report Export feature generates a PDF of the user's vitals, medications, labs, and immunizations. It is gated behind Premium. The individual immunization "Share Record" feature (which generates a single-vaccine certificate PDF) is available to all users without a Premium subscription.

─── REVIEWER CREDENTIALS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

No login is required to use VitalSync. The app is fully functional without an account. All data is stored locally on device. No test account credentials are needed.

To test Premium features, tap any gated feature (e.g., EKG tab, Medications tab) to trigger the paywall, then tap "Start Free Trial." The StoreKit sandbox environment will be used automatically during review.
```

## SECTION 9 — REVIEWER TESTING INSTRUCTIONS

```
REVIEWER WALKTHROUGH — VitalSync 2.9.83

1. ONBOARDING
   • Launch the app. You will be presented with a privacy consent screen.
   • Tap "Begin My Journey" to proceed through onboarding.
   • You will be prompted to authorize HealthKit access. You may grant or deny — the app works either way.
   • After onboarding, you land on the Dashboard (Home tab).

2. STARTING THE FREE TRIAL
   • Tap any Premium-gated tab: Medications, EKG (via the Dashboard EKG card), or Appointments.
   • A bottom sheet appears explaining the feature and offering "Start Free Trial."
   • Tap "Start Free Trial" to initiate the StoreKit sandbox purchase flow.
   • After confirming in the sandbox, Premium features unlock immediately.

3. ACCESSING PREMIUM FEATURES
   • Medications tab: Log a medication, set a schedule, and view adherence tracking.
   • Appointments tab: Add an appointment and set a reminder.
   • Dashboard: Tap and hold any card to enter drag-and-drop customization mode.
   • Analytics tab: View Advanced Correlations between vitals, sleep, and nutrition.

4. TESTING EKG
   • Navigate to the EKG tab (accessible from the Dashboard EKG card or the Record tab).
   • If EKG recordings are present in Apple Health (requires Apple Watch), they will appear as a waveform.
   • If no recordings are present, an empty state with setup instructions is shown.
   • This feature requires Premium — the gate sheet will appear for free users.

5. TESTING PROJECTED A1C
   • Navigate to Log → Blood Sugar.
   • Enter at least one blood glucose reading.
   • The Projected A1C card will appear on the Blood Sugar screen (Premium required).
   • Tap the card to see the estimated A1C trend.

6. TESTING EXPORTS
   • Navigate to the Report tab.
   • Tap "Generate Report" (Premium required).
   • A PDF health report is generated and can be shared via the iOS share sheet.
   • For immunization certificate export: Log → Immunizations → tap a record → Share Record.

7. TESTING SMART REMINDERS
   • Navigate to Log → Medications and add a medication with a scheduled time.
   • A smart reminder notification will be scheduled for the next dose time.
   • Immunization reminders: add a vaccine with a next-dose date within 48 hours to see the dashboard reminder card.
```
