## Description (4,000 characters max)

VitalSync: Clinical Hub is a professional-grade health monitoring app built for patients managing chronic conditions, healthcare professionals tracking patient wellness, and individuals who want clinical-level insight into their own body — not just raw numbers.

**A Correlation Engine Grounded in Science**

Most health apps show you data. VitalSync shows you what your data means. The built-in Correlation Engine applies six validated scientific models to your personal readings — comparing you only to your own baseline, never to population averages:

- **Sleep-Cardiovascular Reciprocity** (Cappuccio et al., 2011): detects when poor sleep efficiency precedes elevated morning blood pressure.
- **Allostatic Load Model**: identifies when your resting heart rate and blood pressure remain elevated during recovery periods, signalling depleted resilience.
- **HRV-Guided Training (Kibler Model)**: uses a rolling 7-day HRV average to flag when your autonomic nervous system needs a recovery day — the same methodology used by Oura and Whoop.
- **72-Hour Lag Analysis** (Mullington et al., 2009): tracks how sleep deprivation on Day 1 correlates with reduced HRV and elevated blood pressure on Day 3.
- **Circadian Rhythm Model**: correlates deep and REM sleep architecture with next-morning fasting glucose and resting heart rate.
- **DASH Sodium Sensitivity** (Sacks et al., 2001): detects whether your sodium intake drives blood pressure spikes and weight fluctuations over 24–48 hours.

Each insight is presented with a clear Hypothesis, an Evidence Level rating based on your own occurrence history, and a nurse-vetted Clinical Suggestion.

**Log Everything — Hands-Free**

VitalSync makes data entry effortless across all four log screens. Press and hold the microphone button and say "my blood pressure was 122 over 78 this morning" — Whisper transcription and AI parsing pre-fill the form instantly. Photograph a food label or meal and the AI vision model extracts sodium and potassium values automatically.

**DASH Dietary Compliance**

Track your daily sodium and potassium intake side by side. The Na:K ratio badge (grounded in INTERSALT trial data) tells you at a glance whether your electrolyte balance is protective or risky. The Dashboard shows a weekly DASH compliance score (0–100) with a 4-week sparkline so you can see whether your dietary habits are improving over time.

**Doctor-Ready Reports**

Generate a comprehensive PDF report covering blood pressure trends, HRV patterns, sleep quality, medication adherence, and a 30-day sodium/potassium table with DASH analysis — formatted for clinical review and ready to share with your physician or care team.

**Your Data Stays on Your Device**

All health data is stored exclusively on your device. VitalSync does not sync your readings to any external server and does not share your data with third parties. You own your data entirely.

**Designed for Clinical Clarity**

VitalSync is built to MSN/RN standards. Every insight references peer-reviewed literature. Every suggestion is framed as a clinical observation, not a diagnosis. The app is a decision-support tool, not a replacement for professional medical care.

## App Store Review Notes (for Apple Review team)

VitalSync: Clinical Hub is a personal health monitoring and correlation analysis app. It does not provide medical diagnoses. All Smart-generated insights include a disclaimer stating they are not medical advice. The app uses the device microphone for voice-to-text data entry (press-and-hold to record, release to transcribe) and the camera for food label scanning. Both features are initiated exclusively by user action. HealthKit integration reads and writes vitals data with explicit user permission. All data is stored locally on the device; no health data is transmitted to external servers.
